## 🗺️ Summary
- Kira, Oly, Z'rak and Valwyn entered Aelinar through crowds fleeing the city and met a priestess of Lunira reuniting two lost children with their parents.
- She begged them to help defend her temple, and they followed her into a battle against invaders disguised as Tyril raiders.
- Kira thought one of the invaders, a winged leader, resembled a fallen angel from depictions of the Twilight Rebellion.
- The raiders escaped into tunnels and sealed them behind, taking the temple's relics. Kira kept one alive.
- Mithaela Carrow, high priestess of the temple, recognized Kira's blade as a Celestium artifact and demanded to know who Kira was.

## ▶️ Where We Started
- Location: the gates of [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar\|Aelinar]]
- Situation: the ground was shaking, the line of travelers was scattering, and the city was in uproar.
- Party goal at the start: find out what was happening in Aelinar.

## 📜 Key Events
1. **Into the city.** The party entered Aelinar against a tide of people fleeing the shaking ground.
2. **The priestess and the children.** A priestess of Lunira was helping two small children find their parents. The party helped reunite them.
3. **A call for help.** The priestess begged the party to come to her temple, which was under attack.
4. **The Moonwater Temple.** The party fought the invaders and noticed their Tyril disguises.
5. **The winged leader.** Kira saw a winged leader among the raiders, and he resembled a fallen angel from depictions of the Twilight Rebellion.
6. **The retreat.** The raiders took the temple's relics, slipped into the tunnels and sealed them behind them. No relics were recovered. Kira kept one raider alive.
7. **A challenge.** Mithaela Carrow recognized Kira's blade as a Celestium artifact but did not recognize Kira. She demanded that the party hand over the survivor for questioning, then asked Kira, "Who are you, and where did you get that…"
