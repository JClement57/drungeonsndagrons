## 🗺️ Summary
- Valwyn Ravara spent the day in Thornbury watching Father Ignatius Greaves, the temple and the village, and saw the party arrive and rob the temple.
- Ignatius found his office and stores raided and rang the alarm bells, threatening to burn Thornbury unless what was taken was returned. Z'rak's illusion of Solarin sent him into a rage.
- The party and Valwyn defeated Ignatius before he could act. He set one building alight as he fell, and the party and the villagers put out the fire.
- Valwyn took a mark from Ignatius's body and explained to the party what marks are. The group set out together for Aelinar, and the ground began to shake as they neared its gates.

## ▶️ Where We Started
- Location: [[Thornbury\|Thornbury]]
- Situation: Valwyn is tracking Father Ignatius and scouting the village on his own, hours before the party arrives.
- Party goal at the start: Valwyn wanted to learn Ignatius's routines and weaknesses.

## 📜 Key Events
1. **Valwyn scouts Thornbury.** He watched Ignatius at the temple and a caravan being readied to carry goods to Aelinar. He chose not to interfere with either.
2. **The tavern.** Valwyn listened in on the Wayrest Inn's conversations and saw the party come in. He overheard Kira and Una talk about Ignatius's gambling and learned why the village feared him.
3. **The heist, seen from the shadows.** Valwyn watched the party sneak into the temple, watched Z'rak pose as Solarin and scold Ignatius, and watched them slip away. He did not interfere.
4. **The alarm.** Ignatius discovered his office and stores had been raided. He rang the alarm bells and threatened to burn the village down unless the stolen goods were returned.
5. **A second Solarin.** Z'rak cast *Major Illusion* and showed an image of "Solarin" to the whole village. Ignatius flew into a rage, and Z'rak and Valwyn attacked.
6. **The fall of Ignatius.** The party defeated him before he could act. As he fell, he set a building alight.
7. **The fire.** The party and the villagers worked together to put it out. Kira pulled a young girl from the burning building. The village thanked them, and Oly gave back as much money as she could to the people of Thornbury.
8. **The mark.** Valwyn sat beside Ignatius's body and performed a ritual that took one of his marks, a fire power that shields him from fire and burns those who strike him. He then explained to the party what marks are.
9. **The road to Aelinar.** The party and Valwyn joined forces. At the gates, in a line of waiting travelers, the earth began to tremble from somewhere inside the city.
