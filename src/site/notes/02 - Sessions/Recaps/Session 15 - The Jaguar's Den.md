## 📜 Key Events
1. Vittorio causing a ruckus at the Sanctuary inn
2. The party heads to and joins the tournament at the Jaguar's Den - Kira, Amaeric and Jin participated
3. Kira was nervous about a Celestium trainer (Dame Oriane Deepcroft) there with her ward and took Z'rak's hat of disguise and turned into "Karl"
	1. Karl ended up winning the tournament!
4. Jin faced off against a young Celestium vanguard-in-training before facing Vittorio who cheated in their bout. Jin was pushed by his newfound patron to use his own magic to fight back against Vittorio's cheating
	1. He didn't, but his honor brought into question by his patron led to him lashing out and getting kicked out of the dojo
	2. Jin challenged Vittorio to an honor duel outside of the dojo where Jin got his booty kicked, but his fight was really with his patron who revealed himself to be Vaelen - "the nothingness between the stars"
	3. Vaelen revealed that Yaretzi Mizuki was still alive and the Jin needed to go find her and help her with what she was working on. Jin left for the Tzicali Isles the next morning and Amaeric joined him
5. Oly and Z'rak chatted with the owner of Francis' Fish House and convinced them to open a franchise in the party's town
