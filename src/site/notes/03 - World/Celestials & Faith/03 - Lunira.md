*The second celestial, born in the Sun’s shadow. She rules all that shifts, fades, and transforms.*

## Overview
- Governs what changes, fades, evolves, or transforms.
- Where [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]] creates and defines, she disturbs and redefines.
- Does not create. She **transforms**.
- Guides endings toward new beginnings.
- Makes room for secrets, chaos, and uncertainty.
- Does not oppose the Sun. She complements him.

## Domain
- **Death:** the dying find passage through her.
- **Sea and storms**
- **Dreams and secrets:** whispered secrets are hers to keep.
- **Deception**
- **The in-between:** life and death, dusk and dawn, silence and song.

## Personality
- Elusive, mercurial, enigmatic.
- Trickster, mourner, and passing storm.
- Speaks in riddles and dreams.
- Loves deeply and mourns forever.
- Especially mourns the stars she once bore from Solarin’s fire.
- Often linked with trickery and darkness.
- Also comforts the grieving and guides souls.
- Her beauty is mesmerizing and terrible: a face you can never see fully.

## Worshippers
- Sailors and storm-callers
- Mourners and gravekeepers
- Spies and assassins
- Dreamers
- Many pray at dusk, especially in grief or fear.

## Relationships
- **[[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]]:** her counterpart. She shapes what he creates.
- **[[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|The Stars]]:** she bore some of them, and mourns them.
