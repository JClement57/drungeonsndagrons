*In Caelthys, the people worship celestials instead of gods. Three tiers rule the cosmos.*

## Overview
- The **Celestial Triad** is the structure of the divine.
- It has three tiers: the Sun, the Moon, and the Stars.
- Together they shape light and shadow, life and death, knowledge and mystery.
- The Triad is about **balance**, not conflict.

## The Three Tiers
| Tier | Celestial | Role |
| ---- | --------- | ---- |
| 1 | [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]], the Sun | Creator. Sets the constants of reality: structure, life, continuity |
| 2 | [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/03 - Lunira\|Lunira]], the Moon | Transformer. Governs change, endings, and the in-between |
| 3 | [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|The Cosmic Legion]], the Stars | Administrators. Each holds one domain, such as war, peace, knowledge, storms, or magic |

## Balance
- Solarin and Lunira are **not** good and evil.
- They are light and dark, constant and change, order and chaos.
- Their push and pull keeps the world moving.
- The Sun sets the **constants**.
- The Moon brings **change**, so the cycle of creation and dissolution continues.
- The Stars tend the **fine details**.
- The Stars serve as children or servants of the Sun and Moon.

## Law
- Solarin made [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/05 - Celestial Law\|Celestial Law]] to keep order in the celestial realm.
- Breaking it means banishment.

## Worship
- Mortals may revere one celestial, a constellation, or the whole Legion.
- [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/06 - The Order of the Celestium\|The Order of the Celestium]] worships all celestials together.
