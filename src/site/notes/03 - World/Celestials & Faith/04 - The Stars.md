*A countless legion of celestials. Each is a steward of one aspect of reality.*

## Overview
- Countless celestial beings, created by the Sun and Moon.
- Servants, champions, and stewards.
- Each holds a **domain**, assigned by the Sun or Moon.
- Some are radiant and noble. Others are subtle and strange.
- Each is a celestial being in its own right.
- Together they are the **Legion**.

## Power
- Their power is **bestowed**, not inherent.
- It reflects the celestials who made them.
- Their authority is limited to their domain.
- They are lesser than Solarin and Lunira.
- They are divine echoes: fragments of greater power.

## Role in the world
- The Sun and Moon rarely appear in the mortal world.
- The Stars act more directly.
- They serve as patrons to clerics, warlocks, and other divine agents.
- Their light is the promise that knowledge and guidance endure in darkness.

## Personality
- They cover the whole range of personality and alignment.
- Some are warm and compassionate. Others are cold and distant.
- A few whisper secrets in dreams. Others inspire inventions or crusades.
- They are watchers and mentors, not creators.
- *Example:* the star of the harvest is joyous and celebratory.
- *Example:* the star of death may be seen as cruel, pulling the threads of fate to expand his domain.

## Law and banishment
- Stars are bound by [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/05 - Celestial Law\|Celestial Law]].
- A star that threatens Solarin’s order risks its place in the sky.
- Banished stars are cursed to live numbered days on the lower planes.

## Worshippers
- Scholars and mages
- Soldiers and monks
- Historians
- Seekers of peace or war
- Some follow a single star. Others revere the whole Legion.
