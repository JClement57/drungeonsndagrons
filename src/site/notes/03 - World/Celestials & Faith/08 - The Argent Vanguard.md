*The armored knights of the Celestium.*

## Overview
- The militant arm of the Celestium.
- Its members are called **Argents**.
- Heavily armored knights.
- Their armor shimmers in the light.
- The highest-ranking officers wear the most ornate armor.

## Duties
- Protect religious facilities.
- Act as "negative reinforcement" against heretical doctrines.

## Ranks
| Rank | Role |
| ---- | ---- |
| 1. Lumen | Squire |
| 2. Vesper | Knight |
| 3. Beacon | Sergeant |
| 4. Constellian | Commands a company |
| 5. Marshal of the Legion | Highest rank. Answers to the High Priest of the Stars |

## Insignia
- A shoulder patch shows rank.
- Low ranks wear a plain shield.
- Middle ranks wear a shield with a star in the center.
- Officers wear full constellation filigree.
- The filigree differs by the officer’s position.
