*The laws that keep order in the celestial realm.*

## Overview
- [[03 - World/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]] wrote the law to keep order among celestials.
- It binds every being in the celestial realm.
- Any breach means **immediate banishment**.
- A banished star keeps only a sliver of its radiant power.
- In effect, it becomes a mortal.

## The Five Laws

### The Law of the Eclipse
- No star may challenge, rival, or try to equal the Sun’s light.
- Doing so is an act of war against the Sun.

### The Law of Appointed Domain
- Each star is assigned a domain, territory, or concept.
- Leaving it without the Sun’s command is forbidden.

### The Law of the Constellation
- Stars must stay in their ordained place in the cosmic order.
- Leaving a constellation without decree is **treason**.

### The Law of the Veil
- Celestial truths are **not for mortal minds**.
- The full knowledge of the heavens must never be revealed.
- Too much divine knowledge can cause madness, heresy, or war.

### The Law of Purity
- No celestial may mingle its essence with mortals or forbidden beings (fiends, undead, aberrations).
- Forbidden acts include:
  - Siring half-celestial children
  - Creating mixed beings
  - Sharing divine power directly
