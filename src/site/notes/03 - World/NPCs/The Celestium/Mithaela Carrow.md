**Mithaela Carrow** is the high priestess of the Moonwater Temple, Lunira's temple on the West bank of [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar\|Aelinar]], and a priestess of [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/06 - The Order of the Celestium\|the Order of the Celestium]].

> [!quote] Mithaela Carrow
> "Who are you, and where did you get that…"

## Description
### Appearance
Mithaela is a human woman in her forties with greying auburn hair. She carries herself with composure, even in the wreck of her temple.

## Biography
### [[02 - Sessions/Recaps/Session 18 - The Moonwater Temple\|Session 18]]: The Moonwater Temple
When raiders disguised as Tyril soldiers struck the Moonwater Temple, a priestess of Lunira brought the party to defend it. Mithaela was the temple's high priestess.

The raiders escaped into their tunnels with the temple's relics and sealed them behind. In the quiet afterward, Mithaela recognized Kira's sword as a Celestium artifact. She didn't recognize Kira. She demanded that the party hand over the one raider they had kept alive for questioning, then turned to Kira and asked who she was and where she had gotten the blade.

## Relationships
### [[04 - Party/Characters/Kira Lux\|Kira Lux]]
Mithaela knew Kira's sword on sight as a Celestium artifact, though she didn't know Kira. Their first conversation ended with her question still unanswered.
