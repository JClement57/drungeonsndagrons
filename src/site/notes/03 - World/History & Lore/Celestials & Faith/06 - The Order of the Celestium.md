*The church of all celestials. It has no crown, but it shapes morality across Caelthys.*

## Overview
- Worships **all** celestials, not individuals.
- Recognizes Solarin as the prime celestial.
- Teaches that Solarin created Lunira and the Stars.
- Has temples across Caelthys.
- Led by [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/07 - The Triumvirate\|the Triumvirate]].
- Its head is the **Polaris**, who is always an Aasimar.

## Doctrine
- Celestials are the ultimate powers in the universe.
- Solarin is the greatest.
- Solarin made Lunira and the Stars to rule over different dominions.
- Celestials can bring good or ill to their domains, depending on their followers’ devotion.

### Reciprocity
- The Order follows a theology of **reciprocity**.
- Worshippers give devotion. Celestials give light, warmth, and stability.
- The Order’s goal is to keep the celestials pleased.
- Clergy are **shepherds** who teach people how to please the celestials.
- Reward: good harvests, calm seas, and more.

## Structure
- **Laity:** ordinary worshippers.
- **Priests:** begin dedicated to one star.
- **Constellation priests:** dedicated to a constellation, a group of stars with related domains.
- **Pantheon priests:** dedicated to the whole celestial pantheon.
- **Leadership:** [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/07 - The Triumvirate\|The Triumvirate]].
- **Soldiers:** [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/08 - The Argent Vanguard\|The Argent Vanguard]].

## Rites and practices
- See [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/09 - Celestium Rites and Practices\|Celestium Rites and Practices]].
- Covers clergy rites, household worship, and giving to the Church.

## Holy symbol
- Centered on Solarin’s symbol.
- Lunira flanks it.
- A ring of constellations surrounds it.

## Politics and influence
- Holds no direct political power.
- Shapes Caelthys’s sense of morality.
- Many rulers bow to it to stay in good standing with their people.
- Many nations fund its presence so its leaders won’t turn their people against them.
- **Exception:** [[03 - World/Locations/The Dulann Confederacy/The Dulann Confederacy\|The Dulann Confederacy]] has rejected its doctrines.
  - Their land is bitterly cold.
  - They believe Solarin never looked upon it.
  - They felt betrayed and now trust their own strength.

## The Constellarium
- See [[03 - World/Locations/Korrandi League/The Constellarium\|The Constellarium]].
- The Order’s seat, in the capital of the Korrandi League.
- A huge, ornate series of buildings.
- Topped by a massive dome.
- The dome holds a magical **Stellarium**, not a telescope.
- Viewers shape the projected stars around them.
- It is used to study the Astral realm.

## Internal debate
- Reformers dislike the idea that money can solve moral problems.
- [[03 - World/NPCs/The Celestium/Tamara Windemere\|Tamara Windemere]] argues for removing indulgences.
- [[03 - World/NPCs/The Celestium/Polaris Aurelian IV\|Polaris Aurelian IV]] and [[03 - World/NPCs/The Celestium/Lathai Torro\|Lathai Torro]] keep them.
- They say indulgences fund the Order and the Vanguard.

## Wealth
- Funded by selling indulgences and religious artifacts.
- Artifacts are kept in homes or used in rituals to gain a celestial’s favor.
- The coffers are vast.
- Ornate temples and a collection of legendary artifacts show its wealth.
