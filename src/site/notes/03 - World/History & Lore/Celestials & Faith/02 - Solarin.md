*The first spark. Creator of the world, the heavens, and time.*

## Overview
- The original force in the universe.
- Shaped reality, the heavens, and time.
- Everything that follows rules and patterns falls under him.
- Seen as the ultimate power in the cosmos.
- Distant, but his light touches all things.
- Rarely intervenes. Works through the [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|Stars]].

## Domain
- **Stability:** day follows night because he commands it.
- **Creation:** his light makes forests, gives life to land, and fuels the crafts of mortals and celestials.
- **Order:** civilization, law, and nature are expressions of his order.
- **Origin of the Stars:** he set the stars in motion and gave them dominion.
- **Author of the law:** he wrote [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/05 - Celestial Law\|Celestial Law]].

## Personality
- Majestic, unwavering, paternal.
- Speaks rarely. His words become law.
- Benevolent but stern.
- Favors those who bring structure to chaos and who build rather than destroy.
- Does not suffer betrayal.
- Shows endless patience to those who strive to improve.
- Expects duty, discipline, and service to the greater good.

## Worshippers
- Clerics and druids
- Blacksmiths and creators
- Judges and rulers
- Farmers, who pray at dawn
- Anyone who seeks the light, literally or spiritually

## Relationships
- **[[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/03 - Lunira\|Lunira]]:** complements him. She reshapes what he creates.
- **[[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|The Stars]]:** his stewards and servants.
