*A fog-choked fishing town on the western coast, leaning toward the sea.*

## Overview
- A modest town on the rocky western coast.
- Weather-worn docks have long been its lifeline.
- Mists roll in from the sea, giving the town its gray look.
- Mostly aging timber buildings stained by salt and damp.
- Moss on stone foundations and sagging rooftops.
- The folk are hardy and quietly hospitable.

## Government
- A small council of elders.
- Headed by **Elder Calwin Dreel**, a retired shipwright.
- The council handles disputes, trade, and local defense.
- The **Town Guard** keeps order, commanded by **Captain Tolen Brask**.
- Brask is respected for being fair but stern.
- Small disputes are settled by custom or Calwin’s mediation.

## Known for
- **Fishing and salted goods:** the main trade. Much of the catch is smoked or salted for sale inland.
- **Ship repairs:** not a shipbuilding center, but docks repair and outfit small vessels.
- **Weathered charm:** visitors note the quiet, eerie beauty of the mists and the people’s resilience.
