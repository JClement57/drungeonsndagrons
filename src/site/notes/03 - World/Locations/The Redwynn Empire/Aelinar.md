**Aelinar** (EH-lih-nar), known as the Twin Thrones, is the capital of the Redwynn Empire. It has a population of approximately 100,000 and is divided by a river and joined by bridges.

## Overview
Aelinar straddles the wide, emerald Virendale River, approximately three miles inland from the western coast. The city was formed by the union of two rival hill-cities, which are now referred to as the western and eastern cities. Bridges, ferries, and floating markets connect the two halves.

## The two halves
The western and eastern cities have distinct economies, temples, and identities. The East is the center of commerce, and the West is the center of craft.

## Population
Aelinar has a population of approximately 100,000, drawn from across the continent and of varying species and origins. Its neighborhoods have distinct characters, and festivals, rivalries, and civic pride unify the city.

## Government
The Redwynn Empire is a monarchy in which the Emperor holds final authority. The Redwynn family is held in high regard by the populace for having liberated the people from the [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/History of the Redwynn Empire#The Condsor Revolution\|Condsor]] dynasty. The Emperor generally leaves towns and commerce to manage themselves through elected representatives, and he consults the public through guild assemblies and petitions. The Emperor resides in [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar/The Twin Keeps\|The Twin Keeps]], the castle on an island in the middle of the river. symbolizing equality to both cities of Aelinar.

The Mayor of Aelinar is appointed by the Emperor. The current mayor, Wendell Dunmere, is a friend of the Emperor's father from the period of the revolution. He chairs the Council of Guildmasters and casts the deciding vote in the event of a tie.

The Council of Guildmasters represents the city's trade and artisan guilds. The guilds contest matters of law and influence, and the mayor mediates between them. The council has six seats.

## Law and defense
The Imperial Army garrisons Aelinar and patrols its streets, assisted by a small civic watch. The Imperial Guard protects the Twin Keeps. Each major Celestium temple maintains its own detachment of Argents. The city has been on alert since the Empire began to mobilize.

## The underworld
Aelinar has an established criminal underworld, organized into two groups that each operate on one side of the river. The **Briar Syndicate** is active in the East. It is composed largely of beastfolk. The **Thieves' Guild** is active in the West. It is associated with burglary, fencing, and forgery. The two groups are understood to observe an arrangement not to operate in each other's territory.

## Religion
Aelinar contains several major temples of the Celestium, which honors three tiers: [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]], [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/03 - Lunira\|Lunira]], and [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|the Stars]]. A senior priest, comparable to a bishop, heads the Order in the Empire. The current bishop is Hadrian Pemberton, whose seat is the Sunspire. For further information, see [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/06 - The Order of the Celestium\|The Order of the Celestium]].

## Points of interest
Two sites are notable throughout the city: [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar/The Twin Keeps\|The Twin Keeps]] and [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar/The Sapphire Walk\|The Sapphire Walk]].

### The Twin Keeps
The Twin Keeps are a pair of stone keeps on an island in the river, joined by an arched hall. The East Keep houses the court and the administration of the Empire, and the West Keep houses the Emperor's household. A single causeway and a guarded water gate connect the island to the city.

### The Sapphire Walk
The Sapphire Walk is the largest bridge across the Virendale River. It is lined with market stalls and hung with lanterns, and it is busiest in the evening. Brass bells mounted on its lantern-posts carry sound along the span, and the bridge is known locally as a place where rumors are exchanged after dark.

### The Sunspire
The Sunspire is the principal temple of Solarin in the Empire. It stands on the highest hill of the East and is built of white stone, with a gilded sunburst at its crown. It is the seat of the bishop.

### The Moonwater Temple
The Moonwater Temple is the temple of Lunira, located on the western bank of the river. Lanterns are released from its steps at dusk and drift across the water. Its priests are associated with festivals and storytelling.

### The Hall of Stars
The Hall of Stars is the temple of the Stars. It is smaller than the other two major temples and is topped by a dome painted to resemble the night sky. A company of Argents is stationed there.

### The Floating Markets
The Floating Markets are a group of barges anchored between the two halves of the city and linked by planks and rope bridges. Eastern traders sell spices and wine, and western smiths and weavers sell steel and cloth. The markets are regarded as neutral ground.

### The Council Hall
The Council Hall is a domed building in which the six guildmasters and the mayor meet around a long oval table. Proceedings are lengthy, and the hall is open to the public on certain days.

### The Racing Quay
The Racing Quay is the stone quay from which the annual boat races between the East and the West start and finish. Both halves of the city gather on its banks on race days, and crews practice there at dawn.

### The Last Lantern
The Last Lantern is a tavern on the eastern harbor, decorated with paper lanterns, that remains open until the last lantern burns out. It is frequented by sailors, caravaners, and merchants.

### The Hammer and Loom
The Hammer and Loom is a tavern on Anvil Hill in the West. Its patrons are largely smiths and weavers, and it is known for dark ale and guild gossip.

### The Briar Tea House
The Briar Tea House is a tea room and bakery on the eastern bank, staffed by beastfolk.

### The Velvet Hare
The Velvet Hare is a gambling hall on the eastern bank. It offers high-stakes games and is frequented by the wealthy.

## Atmosphere
Aelinar is known for its festival colors, spirited debates, and quiet rivalries. The East and the West compete in boat races, street performances, and pranks. The air smells of river lilies and spice carts, and lanterns drift across the water. Taverns line every hill and quay.

## Rumors
Common talk on the Sapphire Walk holds that the war council has argued for days over responsibility for the Meridian Peak. It is also said that the mayor is elderly and that the Emperor has not named a successor to him. Both halves of the city claim they will win the next boat race.
