*The Twin Thrones. The capital of the Redwynn Empire, split by a river and joined by bridges.*

## Overview
- Capital of the Redwynn Empire.
- Straddles the wide, emerald Virendale River.
- About three miles inland from the western coast.
- Joins two rival hill-cities into one.
- Now called the western and eastern cities.
- Bridges, ferries, and floating markets stitch the halves together.

## Population
- About 100,000 people.
- Draws folk from all over the continent.
- Neighborhoods have distinct characters.
- Festivals, rivalries, and civic pride keep the city close.

## Government
- Ruled by **the Emperor**, who oversees imperial matters.
- The Emperor lives in [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Aelinar/The Twin Keeps\|The Twin Keeps]], the castle in the middle of the river.
- **The Mayor of Aelinar** is elected every five years.
- **The Council of Guildmasters** represents trade and artisan guilds.
- The guilds spar over law and influence, so the mayor must balance them.
- Guilds and a democratic public steer the city under the Emperor’s banner.

## Religion
- Aelinar holds major temples of the Celestium.
- They honor the three tiers: [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/02 - Solarin\|Solarin]], [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/03 - Lunira\|Lunira]], and [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/04 - The Stars\|the Stars]].
- A senior priest, similar to a bishop, heads the Order in the Empire.
- See [[03 - World/History & Lore/Celestials & Faith/06 - The Order of the Celestium\|The Order of the Celestium]].

## Atmosphere
- Festival colors, spirited debates, quiet rivalries.
- East and West compete in boat races, street performances, and pranks.
- During the **Luminous Run** the river glows and the city unites.
- Air smells of river lilies and spice carts.
- Lanterns drift across the water.
- Taverns line every hill and quay.
