*A modest open market ringed by wooden stalls.*

## Overview
- A modest open area ringed by wood-plank stalls.
- Busiest on market days.
- Folk from nearby hamlets come to barter.

## Goods
- Smoked fish
- Pickled vegetables
- Rough-spun wool
- Simple wood carvings
- Imported odds and ends from coastal traders
