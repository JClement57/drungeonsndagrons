## Overview
- The largest bridge spanning the two sides of Aelinar.
- Adorned with market stalls.
- Lit by lanterns at night.
- A sight to behold after dark.

## Rumor
- Marlan Rooke, a ferryman, says to stand here after dark if you want to hear secrets.
