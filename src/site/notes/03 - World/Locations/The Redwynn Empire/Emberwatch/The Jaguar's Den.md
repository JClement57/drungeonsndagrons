*A dojo run by an old Tzicali master  in Emberwatch.*

## Overview
A simple dojo in Emberwatch run by [[03 - World/NPCs/Emberwatch/Balam Arakawa\|Balam Arakawa]]. The party encountered it on an off day and Jin's altercations there led to him departing the party and seeking out his own adventure with his past back in [[03 - World/Locations/The Tzicali Isles/The Tzicali Isles\|The Tzicali Isles]].

## The tournament
The Jaguar's Den hosted a simple sparring tournament to raise some awareness of the dojo and to offer a place for aspiring duelists to train and compete. House Onyx often uses these tournaments to train young fighters and to scout new talent. The Celestium was also doing the same for young members of the Vanguard.

The party discovered the tournament because it was advertised on the [[03 - World/Locations/The Redwynn Empire/Emberwatch/Sanctuary Inn\|Sanctuary Inn]] board.
